## 1. Why Digital Business Cards are Dominating Modern Networking

Networking has shifted from passive contact exchange to active lead acquisition. Traditional paper cards suffer from a **90% drop-off rate**—most are thrown away within a week. Modern virtual networking solutions solve this pain point directly.

### Core Value Propositions:
* **Dynamic Content Updates:** Update your phone number, social links, or portfolio instantly without reprinting.
* **Instant Contact Saving (vCard):** Download contact details directly into an iPhone or Android address book with one tap.
* **NFC & QR Code Integration:** Tap an NFC card or scan a custom QR code to view a personalized digital profile.
* **Analytics & ROI Tracking:** Monitor link clicks, profile views, and conversion rates across your sales team.
